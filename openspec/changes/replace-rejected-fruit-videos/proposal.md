# Replace rejected fruit clips and bound the player

## Why
The user explicitly requested removal of the portrait shop and single-banana examples because of layout and demonstration quality, and a replacement sized like the existing videos. This explicit removal supersedes the general gallery-preservation rule for those two IDs only.

## What Changes
Remove banana-ripening and hudson-fruit-shop from the active registry and HTTP routes. Add a new apple-press ID: 30 seconds of native landscape HD apple processing, CC BY-SA 3.0, rendered at 1280×720 with actual apple detections. Keep San Francisco, Shibuya and retail intact. Use a consistent 16:9 player with contain sizing, keeping the full image without stretching/cropping. Preserve original/HUD playback and source modal.

## Impact
No output schema or inference-profile changes. Retired local media/results remain ignored and unreachable through HTTP; historical attribution remains documented. New video derivatives retain CC BY-SA 3.0 independently of MIT project code.
